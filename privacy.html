Privacy Policy

Open Gmail
Chrome Extension

Last updated: May 2026  ·  Version 1.0

This extension does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Ever.

What data is stored

The extension saves exactly two preferences on your device using Chrome's built-in chrome.storage.sync API:

  • url — the URL to open (default: mail.google.com)
  • newTab — whether to open in a new tab (default: true)

These preferences are stored locally in your browser. If you are signed into Chrome, they may sync across your own devices via your Google account — this is standard Chrome sync behaviour outside of this extension's control.

What data is NOT collected

The extension does not collect, store, or transmit:

  • browsing history
  • personal information
  • usage statistics
  • crash reports
  • identifiers of any kind

There are no analytics, no tracking pixels, no third-party SDKs, and no network requests made by this extension.

Permissions

The extension requests a single permission:

  • storage — to save your two preferences above

No host permissions, tab read access, or any other capability is requested.

Open source

This extension is fully open source. You can read every line of code on GitHub and verify these claims for yourself.
